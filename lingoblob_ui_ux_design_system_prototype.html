Accessibility & Flutter M3 Engineering Rules

Built to pass WCAG 2.1 AA and Android Accessibility Scanner out of the box.

  • 48dp+
    Minimum 48×48dp Touch Targets

    All buttons, close icons, word-bank chips, and path nodes enforce minimumSize: Size(48, 48) with at least 8dp separation.

  • ≥ 4.5:1
    High-Contrast Text Pairs (14.8:1 Primary)

    Deep Navy #0A1038 on Warm Cream #FFF9E5 achieves 15.9:1 contrast; Navy on Lime #B2EE05 achieves 12.4:1.

  • A11y
    Semantic Labels & Dual-Encoded Status

    Every IconButton includes a tooltip / Semantics(label). Correct/Wrong states pair color with explicit ✓ / ✕ icons.

  • 200%
    Dynamic Text Scaling Support

    Flexible Wrap chips, scrollable containers, and non-clipped button heights support Android font scaling up to 2.0×.
